# Standard Return and Refund Policy

Policy key: `standard-returns`  
Version: `1.0`  
Effective date: October 16, 2026

We want you to be happy with your purchase. This policy explains when an item
may be returned, which products are excluded, and how an approved refund is
issued.

## Standard 30-day return window (RET-ELIGIBILITY-001)

Most products may be returned within 30 calendar days after the original
purchase date. The return request must identify the original order and the
specific items being returned.

Items returned after the 30-day window are not eligible for a refund under the
standard policy unless another written warranty or applicable law requires a
different result.

## Product condition (RET-CONDITION-001)

The product should be returned in substantially the same condition in which it
was received. All included parts, accessories, manuals, and packaging should be
returned when reasonably possible.

We may reduce or deny a refund when an item is damaged through misuse, is
missing essential components, or cannot be matched to the original purchase.

## Proof of purchase (RET-PROOF-001)

A valid order number or other proof of purchase is required. The purchaser and
the item being returned must match our order records.

## Non-returnable products (RET-EXCEPTION-001)

Gift cards are final sale and cannot be returned, exchanged, or redeemed for
cash except where required by law.

Activated mobile phones, including activated iPhones, cannot be returned under
the standard return policy. A phone that was delivered defective or damaged
may qualify for warranty service or a replacement review instead of a standard
refund.

## Refund amount (RET-AMOUNT-001)

An approved refund is based on the amount actually paid for the eligible items,
including applicable item-level taxes. Original shipping charges are not
refundable unless the return resulted from our shipping error or the product
arrived damaged or defective.

Promotional discounts are allocated across the purchased items. The refund
cannot exceed the net amount paid for the returned items.

## Refund method and timing (RET-PAYMENT-001)

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Processing time
after approval depends on the payment provider and the customer's financial
institution.

Submitting the same return request more than once does not create an additional
refund.

## Review and approval (RET-APPROVAL-001)

Returns may be sent for manual review when required to confirm eligibility,
product condition, account activity, or refund amount. A refund greater than
$500.00 requires manager approval before it can be issued.

## Questions and statutory rights (RET-RIGHTS-001)

This policy does not limit rights that cannot legally be excluded. Customers
who need help with a return should contact customer support and provide their
order number.
